Chaturanga

Chess, the ancient game it came from, and four armies on one board.

Chaturanga is an app for iPhone and iPad. Pick the game you want and start playing: the app chooses an opponent at your level and moves it as you improve.

  • ChessThe classic game.
  • ChaturangaThe ancient Indian game chess came from, with its own pieces and rules.
  • Four-playerFour armies on one board, every player for themselves.
  • Four-player teamsTwo against two.

What is in it

  • A computer opponent for every game, picked for your level.
  • Puzzles, openings and endgames to practise, chosen for you.
  • A review of each finished game.
  • A simpler, picture-based version for children.
  • Everything works without a connection and without an account.
