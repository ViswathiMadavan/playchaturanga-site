Support

Write to support@playchaturanga.com. We read every message and aim to answer within a few days.

The fastest way to report something that went wrong is inside the app: open Me, then Report a problem. It prepares an email with the details we need.

Common questions

How is my opponent chosen?

The app picks a level you can beat about six games in ten and moves it as you improve. Easier and Harder on the home screen shift it one step, and Game options lets you choose any level.

What is my rating?

Each game has its own rating, kept on your device. It moves after every finished game against the computer. The first few games move it faster while the app finds your level.

How are puzzles chosen?

Puzzles have their own rating. Solving one with no wrong move and no hint raises it; a wrong first move or a hint lowers it. The next puzzle is picked close to that rating.

How do I switch games or add one?

Tap a game at the top of the home screen. Add or hide games under Me, then Games shown. A hidden game keeps its rating and history.

How do I set the app up for a child?

Under Me, set Who is playing to A child. The child version uses large picture tiles, shows no rating number, and keeps settings behind a question for a grown-up.

Where are my past games?

Under Me, then My games. Open any game to replay it; chess and chaturanga games also have a move-by-move review.

Does the app need a connection or an account?

No. Everything runs on your device, and there is nothing to sign in to.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the app stores is on your device. Delete single games in My games, or delete the app to remove all of it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I have a new phone. Where is my progress?

Progress is stored on the device and is included in a device backup. If you set up the new phone from a backup of the old one, your games and ratings should come with it.
