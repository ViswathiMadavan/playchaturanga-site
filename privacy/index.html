Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy covers the Chaturanga app for iPhone and iPad and this website. If anything in it is unclear, write to privacy@playchaturanga.com.

  • There is no account. Your games, ratings, progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app has no advertising and does not track you.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on crash reports or choose to email us.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following on your device and nowhere else:

  • The games you play and their reviews.
  • Your rating in each game, your puzzle rating, and your progress in puzzles, lessons, openings and endgames.
  • Your settings, including which games you chose and whether the app is set up for a child or a grown-up.
  • Problem reports you have written but not sent.

The app asks for no name, email address, birth date, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. All play, including the computer opponent and game review, runs on the device.

What can leave your device

Crash reports, only if you turn them on. The setting "Share crash reports" is off unless you switch it on. When it is on and the app crashes, a report is sent through Firebase Crashlytics, a service run by Google. A report contains technical details of the crash, your device model, system version, app version and an identifier for the app installation. It does not contain your name, email address or your games. Google processes these reports for us under its own terms and keeps them for a limited period (90 days at the time of writing). You can switch the setting off at any time in the app.

Problem reports and emails, only if you send them. "Report a problem" in the app prepares an email in your own mail app. It contains what you wrote and, if you leave "Attach device details" on, your device model, system version, app version and the app's recent activity log. Nothing is sent until you press Send. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use them only to answer you and fix the problem.

Information from Apple. Apple gives app developers combined figures such as download counts and crash counts, and only from people who agreed in their device settings to share them with developers. These figures do not identify anyone.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No usage analytics: the app does not report what you tap or play.
  • No accounts, sign-in or online play in this version.
  • No purchases in this version.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for advertising. The only outside party that processes any data from the app is Google, for crash reports, and only when you have turned them on.

Children

Children can use the app, and it has a version designed for them. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children, or from anyone: it works without an account and asks for no personal details. In the child version, settings, links that leave the app and sending email sit behind a question meant for a grown-up. "Share crash reports" is off unless a grown-up switches it on.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, write to privacy@playchaturanga.com and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Crash reports: switch "Share crash reports" on or off in the app at any time.
  • Your games and progress: delete single games in the app's game list, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.
  • Emails you sent us: ask us to delete them at privacy@playchaturanga.com.

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to see, correct or delete personal information held about you, and to object to how it is used. Because the app keeps your data on your device and we hold no account for you, the only personal information we can hold is email you sent us. To use any of these rights, write to privacy@playchaturanga.com; we answer within 30 days.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare, which, like any web host, process technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages. Email to our addresses is forwarded by Cloudflare to our inbox.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts to handle data differently, for example if accounts, online play or purchases are added, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
